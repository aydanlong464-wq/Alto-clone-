# ASHFALL // DESCENT

A physics-based endless downhill runner. Flat cel-shaded silhouettes, hard rim light, and bright saturated skies that fall off into black.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install or build.

To play it from a link, turn on GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root**. The game then runs at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tap / Space / ↑ | Jump |
| Hold in the air | Backflip |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute |

Land with your board matching the slope or you're dead.

## What's in it

- **Physics.** Momentum carries you along the slope. You launch off crests and ramps on your own speed, gravity pulls you down, and landings convert your velocity back onto the slope.
- **Tricks.** Backflips (double and triple count), perfect landings, big air, and grinding along power lines. Tricks build a combo multiplier and give you a speed boost.
- **Hazards.** Rocks to jump, chasms to clear, cliffs to drop.
- **Zones.** Every 1000 m the world shifts palette: Crimson, Sulfur, Cyanide, Nerve, Acid, Ember.
- **Look.** Halftone skies, posterized terrain bands, film grain, scanlines, letterbox HUD, hard-cut trick callouts.
- **Sound.** Synthesized live with WebAudio: wind, board carve, a drone that changes key per zone, and trick stings.

Your best score is saved in the browser.
